# 10 MILLION DOWNLOADS
We just passed 10 million downloads on PortMaster!
We wanted to take a moment to say thank you to everyone who got us here.

<!-- more -->

Maybe you've been testing builds, reporting bugs, porting games, or just playing on your handheld after a long day. Whichever it is, you've been part of this, and we really appreciate it.

A big thank you to our Crew, Port Engineers, Port Cadets and our testers, the Port Navigators. You're the backbone of this project. The time and patience you put in have brought so many games to life on hardware they were never meant for.

This isn't only a look back at what we've done. Big things are coming, and we're already thinking about the next 10 million. In the video you'll also get a sneak peek at some potential upcoming ports, and we're sharing a few new things we've been working on.

**Thanks for being here with us.**

We put together a video to mark the moment, so go check it out!
